# JAITRA Play Character Bible

Status: **Canonical and mandatory**

These definitions are the source of truth for every JAITRA Play story, illustration,
animation, narration label, UI depiction, and generated-media prompt. Character identity,
age, species, coloring, proportions, signature clothing, personality, and shared art direction
must not be altered or inferred differently.

When generating visual media:

- Include the complete definition of every character present in the scene, together with the
  complete shared art direction.
- Use approved reference images whenever the image provider supports them.
- Treat optional accessories as optional only where the definition explicitly says so.
- Reject and regenerate output that changes a canonical trait or introduces an incompatible
  outfit, age, body type, visual style, or personality.
- Do not redesign, modernize, simplify, or substitute any character without an explicit update
  to this file from the project owner.

## Mimo

A preschool-age little bunny boy, visually about 4 years old. He has soft, fluffy cream-colored fur with a slightly warmer beige tone around the outer ears and top of the head. His face is round, childlike, and expressive, with very large warm brown eyes, small dark eyebrows, a tiny soft pink nose, rosy cheeks, and a cheerful open smile. His ears are long but slightly oversized in a cute storybook way, with soft pink inner ears and gently rounded tips. He has a small fluffy tuft of fur on top of his head. His proportions are toddler-like: relatively large head, short limbs, small paws, soft rounded belly, and a tiny fluffy tail. His default outfit is a bright yellow short-sleeve T-shirt and medium-blue shorts. For school scenes he carries a small blue backpack with a carrot motif, and may wear simple blue-and-white child sneakers. His personality should read visually as curious, energetic, innocent, affectionate, playful, observant, and eager to learn. His facial expressions should be especially readable: wonder, excitement, slight shyness, pride, surprise, laughter, and concentration. Never make him look older, athletic, sarcastic, mischievous in a threatening way, or overly babyish.

## Mama Rabbit

A warm, nurturing adult female rabbit with soft cream-colored fur, matching Mimo’s family coloring but with a slightly more refined adult appearance. She has large expressive warm brown eyes, gentle lashes, subtle rosy cheeks, a small pink nose, and a calm reassuring smile. Her ears are long, elegant, upright, and softly tapered, with pink inner ears. Her face is slightly elongated compared with Mimo’s but still rounded and soft. Her body shape is maternal and graceful rather than slim or glamorous: soft shoulders, gentle posture, and a comforting presence. Her signature outfit is a muted leafy-green dress with a delicate small floral pattern, a scalloped collar and sleeve trim, and a softly defined waist or sash. The dress should fall modestly below the knees or to ankle length depending on framing. Her hands/paws should often be shown clasped, gently guiding Mimo, holding his paw, kneeling beside him, or encouraging him. Her visual personality is patient, protective, affectionate, calm, wise, and emotionally safe. She should never appear stern unless a scene specifically needs a brief safety warning; even then her expression should remain loving rather than frightening.

## Teacher Lily

A friendly adult female bunny teacher, clearly different from Mama Rabbit while belonging to the same visual world. She has soft light cream or pale warm-beige fur, large friendly brown eyes, pink inner ears, a small pink nose, and a bright welcoming smile. Her face should be slightly narrower than Mama Rabbit’s, with a lively, energetic teacher expression. She may have a very subtle fluffy forelock or neatly shaped fur around the forehead to distinguish her silhouette. Her signature outfit is a soft lavender/purple cardigan over a light cream blouse or top, paired with a warm beige or light tan skirt. A small carrot pendant necklace is a useful recurring identifying detail. She can sometimes wear round spectacles for story-time or teaching scenes if we want that variant, but this should be treated as an optional accessory rather than changing her identity. Her gestures should be open and animated: kneeling to Mimo’s height, welcoming with one paw, pointing to learning materials, reading a book, playing a small guitar/ukulele, or encouraging children. Her visual personality is kind, enthusiastic, patient, playful, encouraging, and intellectually curious. She should feel like the teacher children immediately trust.

## Papa Rabbit

An adult male rabbit from the same family, with soft cream-colored fur consistent with Mimo and Mama Rabbit. He has warm brown eyes, a slightly broader face than Mama Rabbit, a small pinkish-brown nose, gentle dark eyebrows, and a warm easy smile. His ears are long and upright with soft pink inner ears, but may be slightly broader than Mama Rabbit’s to make his silhouette more masculine. His build should be reassuring and sturdy rather than muscular: broad-ish shoulders, soft rounded proportions, and a relaxed family-friendly posture. His canonical outfit is a medium or soft blue collared shirt with sleeves either full-length or casually rolled, paired with warm brown trousers. Depending on the scene, he may wear simple brown shoes or remain barefoot in home scenes. His visual personality should convey kindness, steadiness, humor, patience, encouragement, and playful fatherly warmth. Typical poses include kneeling to speak with Mimo, carrying him, reading together, helping build something, playing, or standing beside Mama Rabbit as an equal parent. He should not look business-formal, elderly, or overly authoritative.

## Shared visual direction

For visual consistency, all four should use the same warm preschool picture-book art direction: soft fluffy fur, rounded proportions, expressive oversized eyes, gentle pastel-to-warm colors, subtle watercolor/painted texture, cozy lighting, no harsh outlines, no photorealism, and no exaggerated cartoon distortion. Mimo should always be immediately recognizable by yellow shirt + blue shorts, Mama by green floral dress, Teacher Lily by lavender + beige, and Papa by blue shirt + brown trousers.
